1. REMOTE WORK
Employees are allowed to work remotely 3 days a week. The designated remote days are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are mandatory in-office days for "Collaboration Syncs."

2. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS
All travel expenses must be approved by a manager if they exceed $200. Coffee and lunch are NOT reimbursable unless it is a client meeting. The reimbursement payout happens on the 15th of every month.

3. UNLIMITED PTO
We offer "Unlimited PTO," but it is subject to approval. You must request leave at least 2 weeks in advance for any trip longer than 3 days.
